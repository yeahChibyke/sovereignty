Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is cNGN?
cNGN is a regulated Nigerian Naira stablecoin — 1 cNGN is always equal to 1 NGN. It's issued by a licensed entity and can be purchased from exchanges or via bank transfer. Sovereignty uses cNGN as the sole collateral and settlement currency.
How do I get cNGN?
You can purchase cNGN from supported exchanges or mint it directly via the cNGN platform using a bank transfer. We'll provide step-by-step guides when the app launches.
What pairs can I trade?
At launch, Sovereignty will support BTC/cNGN and ETH/cNGN perpetual futures. More pairs will be added based on community demand and oracle availability.
What happens if my position gets liquidated?
If your margin ratio falls below the maintenance threshold, your position is automatically closed by the protocol. Any remaining collateral is returned to you. Liquidation bounties go to vault depositors — not to MEV bots.
Is Sovereignty custodial? Who holds my funds?
No. Sovereignty is 100% non-custodial. Your funds are held in open-source smart contracts on Base — not by any company or individual. You maintain full control of your assets at all times.
What is commit-reveal and why does it matter?
Commit-reveal is a two-step process that prevents front-running. First, you submit an encrypted order (commit). Then, you reveal it. This means no one — including validators — can see your trade before it's locked in, protecting you from MEV extraction.
