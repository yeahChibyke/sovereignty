FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sovereignty?
Sovereignty is a trading platform where you can bet on the price of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana going up or down — all using cNGN (digital Naira) as your currency. No broker, no middleman, you trade directly from your own wallet.
What is cNGN?
cNGN is a digital version of the Nigerian Naira — 1 cNGN always equals ₦1. Think of it like having Naira in a digital wallet that works on the internet. It's the currency you use to trade on Sovereignty.
How is Sovereignty different from a broker?
With a broker, they hold your money and can freeze your account anytime. With Sovereignty, your funds stay in your wallet at all times. No one — not even us — can touch your money, block your trades, or manipulate prices.
What do I need to start trading?
Just a crypto wallet (like MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet) and some cNGN. Connect your wallet, deposit cNGN, and you're ready to trade. That's it.
What can I trade?
Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana — all paired with cNGN. You can go long (bet the price goes up) or short (bet it goes down), with up to 5x leverage to amplify your position.
How are my orders protected?
When you place a trade, it's encrypted first so nobody can see it and front-run you. Only after your order is locked in does it get revealed and executed. This means no one can jump ahead of your trade to profit at your expense.
Do I control my money at all times?
Yes, always. Your funds sit in a transparent, verifiable vault — not in someone's bank account. Sovereignty cannot access, freeze, or seize your money. You can withdraw anytime.
What if my trade goes badly?
If the market moves too far against you and your deposit can no longer cover the loss, your position is closed automatically to prevent further losses. This happens transparently using real-time price data — no manual intervention, no surprises.
