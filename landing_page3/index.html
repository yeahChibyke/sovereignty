How It Works

Two flows. One Sovereignty settlement layer.

Traders open and manage cNGN-margined positions. LPs deposit cNGN into market-specific vaults that settle the other side of the trade.

Trader Flow

01
Bring cNGNConnect a wallet and choose how much cNGN to use as collateral.
02
Pick market, side, leverageGo long or short, then set leverage up to the market limit.
03
Commit and executeThe order is committed first, then revealed for execution against a cNGN mark price.
04
Manage or closeAdd collateral, reduce exposure, or close the position when your setup is done.

LP Flow

01
Choose a market vaultSelect the market you understand and want to underwrite.
02
Deposit cNGNReceive vault shares tied to that market's isolated liquidity pool.
03
Back trader settlementThe vault handles trader wins, losses, collateral, and live PnL accounting.
04
Withdraw when readyRedeem shares using the vault's current cNGN-backed accounting.

Protocol FAQ

Questions traders actually ask.

Simple answers on cNGN, wallets, long and short positions, order protection, liquidations, and fund control.

What is Sovereignty?

Sovereignty is a perpetual trading protocol where traders use cNGN as collateral, margin, and settlement for global market exposure.

What can I trade?

The protocol is designed for multiple markets across crypto, commodities, metals, equities, and forex.

What is cNGN?

cNGN is a digital Naira stablecoin. On Sovereignty, it is the currency traders use for collateral, margin, PnL, and settlement.

What do I need to start trading?

You need a compatible crypto wallet and cNGN. Connect your wallet, choose a market, pick long or short, set collateral and leverage, then submit the trade.

How is Sovereignty different from a broker?

Sovereignty is built around transparent smart contracts instead of a broker ledger. Trades, vaults, collateral, and settlement rules are verifiable on-chain.

How are my orders protected?

Orders use a commit-reveal flow. Your trade intent is committed first and revealed later, making it harder for others to see the full order and jump ahead of it.

What if my trade goes badly?

If your position no longer has enough equity to meet margin requirements, it can be liquidated automatically to protect the market from bad debt.

Do I control my money?

You interact from your own wallet. Funds used in trading or LP positions move through transparent protocol vaults rather than a private company balance sheet.
